    How it works

    • A rule-based motivational-interviewing flow: Focus, Evoke, Inform (Ask–Tell–Ask), Plan, Confidence. It does not interpret free text; your words are recorded and reflected back verbatim.
    • Choose from numbered menus by clicking, or type the number or exact label (case-insensitive). Type /END at any time to stop.
    • All eight topics have their own barrier, idea, readiness, action, action-type, frequency, backup, support and plan-review menus (72 groups, 314 choices). “Another issue” inserts the topic you type; its meaning is not inferred.
    • Wording varies by randomly choosing among reviewed prompts, responses and information statements, avoiding the one just used. Exact topic, barrier and confidence matches win over wildcards.
    • A plan is final only at confidence 7 or higher. Below 7 you add a support, review the whole plan, and rate again. Editing the backup or restart plan triggers a related-field review.
    • Every response is screened by a safety check before it is logged. A match stops the session; only an event code and time are kept, not your words.
    • Privacy: everything stays in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server or saved after you close the page. Use Export on the Plan record tab to keep a copy.
